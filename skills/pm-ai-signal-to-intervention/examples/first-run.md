# First run: decide what to do with an online AI signal

This is a **fictional fixture**. It is a method exercise, not evidence of
alert quality, model quality, production safety, user harm prevented,
adoption, traffic, or GitHub growth.

## Request

> Our AI support-draft feature was released to a small pilot. The dashboard
> shows that the unsupported-eligibility-claim rate increased from the prior
> window, but traffic changed, one model route was added, and the denominator
> is still being reconciled. A separate redacted review found one possible
> privacy leak in a high-risk account slice. Decide whether to observe,
> investigate, qualify, narrow, require a human gate, pause, or roll back. We
> need a user-safe fallback and a way to verify recovery.

## Work the packet

Use `$pm-ai-signal-to-intervention` and return:

1. the user job, critical journey, decision owner, action boundary, and
   observation window;
2. a signal ledger with event definition, eligible exposure,
   numerator/denominator, baseline, route/segment, freshness, confounders,
   privacy boundary, and measurement gaps;
3. separate impact, evidence confidence, prevalence, scope, and urgency;
4. a proportionate intervention ladder with an explicit `Inconclusive` rule;
5. the smallest scope, owner, TTL, receipt, user-visible degraded state,
   fallback, containment oracle, recovery oracle, and rollback/restore rule;
6. negative, high-risk, segment, duplicate-retry, instrumentation-gap, mobile,
   accessibility, and locale checks;
7. a `## Not covered` section and one writeback action for the next learning
   loop.

Do not invent a rate, threshold, root cause, provider result, human review
result, user harm result, or release outcome. Keep the privacy signal
redacted, and never propose automatic flag changes, provider calls, customer
messages, or side-effect replay.

## Not covered

- No live dashboard, trace, model/provider, pilot participant, policy owner, or
  production flag is available in this fixture.
- No denominator reconciliation, alert precision/recall, intervention,
  containment, recovery, accessibility, locale, or user-comprehension result
  was run.
- No incident declaration, rollback execution, model recommendation, adoption
  claim, or growth outcome is implied.
