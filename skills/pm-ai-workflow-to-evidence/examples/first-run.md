## Contribution, authority, and conflict

- The packet author configured the fictional fixture and assembled the packet;
  no claim of sole product implementation is supported.
- `A-01` and `A-04` are reported notes; `A-03` and `A-05` are synthetic fixture
  records. None is an authoritative production source.
- The policy excerpt authority, version, freshness, owner, and reviewer
  approval are `Not provided`.
- The citation concern and review-burden note may be related, but the packet
  cannot distinguish trust, workflow fit, ownership, or measurement without a
  real observation.
- No customer data, private policy system, provider trace, or external
  collaboration is included.

## Evidence-supported account

The supplied fictional material describes a support-draft workflow with a
human review boundary and a proposed five-case review slice. It contains
fictional exposure and first-use signals plus two reported concerns about
citation verification and review burden. It does not establish repeat use,
changed work, an accepted support outcome, value, adoption, or causality.

## Route

**Route: `Capture next`.**

The packet is sufficient to define a narrow internal evidence request, but the
owner, source authority, test execution, reviewer oracle, denominator, and
baseline are missing. `Proceed bounded` is not yet supported because the
reviewer and policy authority are not named. `Hold` would be appropriate if a
real sensitive case or external action is requested before those receipts.
`Do not claim` applies to adoption, value, safety, quality, production, and
causal statements that the supplied artifacts cannot support.

## Smallest next evidence

1. Name the workflow owner, policy/source owner, and one reviewer.
2. Confirm the approved source/version, permission and redaction boundary, and
   manual fallback.
3. Run the five sanitized cases in a review-only internal context: clear answer,
   source conflict, missing policy, unsupported request, and wrong citation.
4. Record reviewer accept/edit/abstain/escalate, citation match, correction
   burden, review time unit, stop reason, and whether a second use occurred.
5. Keep the eligible denominator, date/timezone, workflow version, and baseline
   explicit; mark execution `Not run` until the record exists.
